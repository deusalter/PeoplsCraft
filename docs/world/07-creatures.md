# 7. Creatures

This chapter covers everything that moves and is neither a householder nor one of the eight: the kinds met in the open, the ones that come only in attacks, the ones that come up only beside their strongest, and the ones that form on a totem. Each is called by its internal key. Section numbers beginning with 1 point to the ground chapter.

Each creature is given one habit the game could show with a single rule of behaviour. Habits marked *in play* exist today. The rest are *proposed*.

## 7.1 Three kinds of thing

Everything here is an ordinary animal, a person of the sand country, or a feeder. Five are animals, three are people, thirty are feeders.

**The two ranks.** Two ranks of feeder exist on this ground (1.2). The lower has no lasting shape and forms round whatever it is feeding on. What is seen is the thing it is feeding on, moving by itself: a live coal, a crystal, a lump of jelly, a fungus cap. Round it, for about an arm's length, the outlines of things are soft, as they are over a hot stone. `cinder_wisp`, `aether_wisp`, `gelcap`, `sporeling` and everything that forms on a totem are of this rank. Kill one and the thing it was feeding on is what lies on the ground. The higher rank holds a body.

**Shape and matter.** A body that is held has a shape and is made of some matter. The matter is what the animal has been eating, and it is what the animal drops (1.6). The shape belongs to the kind, and its young come up in it. The game draws five shapes: a low crawler on six or eight legs, a four-legged runner, a flier, an upright walker, and the long body of `boss:2`. One shape is filled with different matter in different places, which is why a `crag_bat`, an `umbral_moth` and a `rift_ray` are plainly one make of animal.

**The upright ones.** Eight feeders stand on two legs, and the game calls one of them a knight. None has a self or speech, and none carries, wears or uses anything. What looks like a helmet, a cloak or a blade is body. None is a likeness of a person, and art should give none of them a face or hands. They stand for three plain reasons.

- Growth that stands. `thornling` and `sproutling` are bodied in sapling and bramble, `bog_lurker` in reed and peat, `shardling` in a crystal spire. They stand as those things stand.
- Stored harness. `rustbound` took its body from armour that stood on a frame in a store, and stands as it stood.
- The rest are drawn upright by the game: `gloomshade`, `moonspawn` and `mycelid_knight`. The shade-kind is the kind that comes to the dead, and people are among the dead it has eaten, so there is long bone in it. Nothing more is claimed for its shape.

**Throwing.** Four feeders strike from a distance: `cinder_wisp`, `rustbound`, `umbral_moth` and `aether_wisp`. Each throws a piece of its own matter: ember, rust, dust, crystal. Every throw is a small loss of body, so the throwers are thin and stay back.

**What a feeder wants with a person.** Food. That is the flesh, and it is the lees the person carries, which in any quantity is a store on legs. A feeder also softens what is built. On ground that lies over the root that is too slow to show in a night, and no creature in play harms a wall.

**Coin.** Every feeder killed pays lees, with two exceptions. The small ones that come up beside `boss:1` and `boss:2` have fed on nothing yet and boil down to nothing. What forms on a totem is never killed for its remains.

**Light.** Light on a place stops feeders coming up there, and does not turn one that is already up (1.6). Three kinds are about on the surface at noon, having come up in the dark: `gelcap`, `thornling` and `bog_lurker`. Full sun undoes two kinds, `gloomshade` and `moonspawn`. By the same cause it should undo `lunar_howler`, which in play it does not (chapter 8, changes).

**Hosts.** There are eight kinds of host, one behind each of the eight (1.6). A host is a following and holds kinds of more than one shape. A bait is made of the remains of a host's lesser animals, and the animals that come to a failing strongest are its own, so those two things fix some kinds to a host. The rest are placed by where they feed, and less surely.

| Host of | Feeds on | Fixed by a bait or a fight | Placed by where they feed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `boss:1` | Green growth, standing and fallen | `gelcap`, `sproutling` | `thornling`, `bog_lurker` |
| `boss:2` | Stone, which they leave as sand above and as molten rock below | `cinder_wisp`, `dune_mite` | |
| `boss:3` | Anything dead, and what is boiled down from it | `gloomshade` | |

| `boss:7` | Other feeders | `moonspawn`, `lunar_howler` | |

`frost_stalker`, `rustbound` and `cave_skitter` are placed with no host. The lowest rank runs in no host of its own. The ones named above are counted with the host on whose ground they feed.

## 7.2 Ordinary animals

Five animals of this ground. They belong to no host, pay no lees and want nothing with a person. Struck, one runs (*in play*). Wards mean nothing to them. The four on land are out by day and gone at dusk.

**`woolback`.** A horned grazer in a heavy coat, in herds of two to four on grass and in open wood. Meat and hide. Habit: in the last light the herd walks toward the nearest lamp and lies down inside its light, in an empty village if one is near.

**`gobblet`.** A knee-high ground bird of woods, grass and wet country. Meat and feathers. Its feathers fletch arrows, and the kite that baits `boss:5`. Habit: it calls all day and stops when a feeder is within thirty strides.

**`burrowhog`.** A tusked rooter of cold woods, crags and snow country. The most meat of the five, and a hide. Habit: at dusk it goes down a scrape and stops the hole behind it.

**`dune_skipper`.** A small long-eared runner of the sand country and the dry grass. It lived on the burrower's ground before any person did. Habit: it crosses sand in short runs and stands still between them.

**`glimmerfin`.** A bright fish in any water at any hour. No feeder comes up in water (3.1), so it shares its country with none. Habit: the shoal turns as one (*in play* as grouping).

## 7.3 Feeders

### The woodland host: green growth

**`gelcap`.** No lasting shape. It forms in the jelly that rots out of fallen growth, and what is seen is a knee-high lump of that jelly under a red skin, moving in hops. Grass, wood, mire and dry grass. It hops at a body that comes within fourteen strides. It leaves `gel`, which is the jelly, and that goes into the bait for `boss:1`. Habit: it sits on one patch until disturbed, and the tall grass under it is gone when it moves.

**`thornling`.** A waist-high body of bramble and sapling with a red bud for a crest. Woods, wet woods and mire. It leaves `thorn` and `fiber`. After `boss:1` is killed its packs go for stores at dusk and make a quarter of every `raid`. Habit: by day it stands rooted among bushes and is taken for one. It walks at night, or when a body comes within fourteen strides.

**`bog_lurker`.** The same kind grown old in mud: chest-high, reed and peat, with roots trailing. Mire only. It comes up in mud and lies in it, never in open water. It leaves `fiber`, the `bone` of what died where it lay, and sometimes `bog_pearl`, a lump of lees set in the mud (3.2), which it swallowed. Habit: it lies sunk with the head showing and stands when a body comes within reach.

**`sproutling`.** The least of the host. Four come up at the stone when `boss:1` is half spent, and more after. A badly hurt strongest is about to be food, and its own small ones close in. They have fed on nothing and leave nothing. Habit: when their strongest goes back down they go with it (*in play*; rule 1 of feeders).

### The sand host: stone

**`cinder_wisp`.** No lasting shape. It forms round a coal of the stone its kind has been feeding on, and what is seen is a live coal in the air. In the deep rock it hangs by molten rock at any hour. On sand it comes up at night. It also comes to warm ash, so the fires of the sand-country people have wisps over them. It throws embers from ten strides. It leaves `ember_dust`, which burns twice as long as coal and goes into the lure for `boss:2`. Habit: it does not leave molten rock until a body comes within twenty strides.

**`dune_mite`.** A plated six-legged crawler of grit that lives between the burrower's plates and is shed when `boss:2` thrashes. It leaves nothing. Habit: when the burrower goes under, the mites run to the place where it will come up, and are there first.

### The shade-kind: anything dead

**`gloomshade`.** The commonest thing in the dark. Upright, dark, the height of a person, bodied in bone and in the dark matter of other feeders. It comes to anything dead: a carcass, a spent charge, unsalted lees, a body left on land. That is why it breeds in the broken stores (`ruin`) and why it is most of every `raid`. It took its body in the hollow, went up into the living ground, and none is left below (3.4). On the living ground it comes up everywhere at night and in caves at any hour. Full sun takes it apart in four breaths. It leaves `shade_essence`, which is its own matter raw, and `bone`. Both go into the bait for `boss:3` and into every totem. Four come up beside `boss:3` when it is nearly spent. Opened over a pot, one has a long bone in it of a kind nothing that grazes here has. Habit: among several people it goes first for the one carrying the most lees.

### The hollow host: fungus

All three are up at all hours, since the hollow has no sun.

**`sporeling`.** No lasting shape. It forms in a fungus cap, and what is seen is a lit cap that hops. It leaves `spore_sac` and sometimes the `glowshroom` itself. Three sacs make the bait for `boss:4`. Habit: when one is struck, every sporeling within hearing comes.

**`mycelid_knight`.** Fungus grown two strides tall, with a cap at the top, plates of `umbrite` it has taken up from the rock, and one limb grown out into an edge. It breeds in the first comers' vaults in the hollow (`cache1`), where the lees was. It leaves `spore_sac`, `umbrite_ore` and `bone`. Habit: the edged limb hangs and drags as it walks. It never sets anything down or picks anything up.

**`glowmaw`.** A long-jawed runner, lit along the back and tail. It feeds on sporelings on sufferance and glows with what it has eaten. It leaves `hide`, `spore_sac` and sometimes `noctrium_ore`. Habit: the light on its tail goes out a breath before it runs in.

### The flying host: the air

A feeder that never lands crosses any ward (rule 6). That is why this host's strongest is baited with a kite. The kite is fletched with bird feather so that it flies, and what it carries up is moth dust.

**`umbral_moth`.** The flier of the hollow, bodied in the dust that falls from the great caps. It throws dust from nine strides and leaves `moth_dust`. Habit: it circles above the giant caps and is never seen on the ground.

**`rift_ray`.** The same shape on the islands, broad and scaled, with a tail. It feeds on the undersides of the pale sheets and on what falls from island rims. It leaves `rift_scale`. Habit: it rides under an island's rim and comes up over the edge. It is seldom over the middle.

**`aether_wisp`.** No lasting shape. It forms round old crystal in the air over the islands, and what is seen is a bright crystal moving by itself. It throws crystal from twelve strides. It leaves `mana_crystal` and now and then `rift_scale` from a dead ray it has been at. Habit: it hangs beside the nearest geode, so a wisp marks crystal.

**`crag_bat`.** The same shape on the living ground, bodied in hide and feather from the birds and small animals it takes. Caves at any hour, and the air over crags and peaks at night. It roosts, so a ward turns it like anything that walks. It leaves `feather` and `hide`. Habit: it hangs under an overhang and drops when a body passes beneath.

### The island host: standing crystal

**`shardling`.** Splintered crystal standing like a spire, a person's height and twice as broad, slow and very hard. The surface of the islands. Two come up beside `boss:6` when it is nearly spent. It leaves `shard` and sometimes `prismite_ore`. Three shards make the bait for `boss:6`. Habit: it stands still among the spires and cannot be told from one until it moves.

### The deep kind: other feeders

**`moonspawn`.** Upright, taller than `gloomshade`, giving a pale violet light. It is of the kind that lives in the deepest part of the root and feeds on other feeders. Its body is lees set hard and pale (1.6). It reaches the surface only on the dark night once `boss:3` is dead, and beside `boss:7` and `boss:8`. It is never met in caves or in the hollow, because it goes only where its strongest goes. It leaves `shade_essence`, from what it has eaten, and `moon_sliver`, a bit of its own body. The born say the slivers are pieces of the moon. Habit: it does not go down before dawn, and the sun takes every one still standing (*in play*). This kind has never had to learn the sun.

**`lunar_howler`.** The deep kind's flier, in the air on the dark night only, and the fastest creature in the game. It leaves `moon_sliver`. Habit: it calls before it stoops, and on that evening the call is heard before anything is seen.

### The stone-eaters: rock

**`stonegnawer`.** A plated crawler that feeds on rock, and on coal where it finds it. Its plates are scale of the stone it has eaten. It is the commonest feeder underground, from just above the wood to just under the turf. It breeds in the sealed stores (`dungeon`), where it has been at the dressed stone, and its strongest lies in the base of the eighth kind of great stone (4.10). It leaves `chitin`, which is the plate, and `coal`. Habit: it is found with its head against a wall or a coal seam, and its grinding carries through rock before a miner breaks through to it.

### Kinds placed with no host

**`frost_stalker`.** A pale four-legged runner, bodied in the hide of what it has run down. Snow country, cold woods and peaks, at night, in ones to threes. It took its body on the cold ground of the living ground, under the sun, and the sun does it no harm. It sees a body at twenty-four strides. It leaves `hide` and `frost_fang`, a tooth. Habit: one alone follows at the edge of sight and closes when a second arrives or the quarry is hurt.

**`rustbound`.** Found only in the sealed stores. The lees was the first thing eaten there. This kind then ate the stored harness and arrows, and it stands as the harness stood on its frame. It throws bolts of rust from twelve strides. It leaves `rust_core`, `iron_bar` and `barbed_arrow`. Opened over a pot it has no bone anywhere in it. The born say it was posted there. It nests on its food. Habit: it does not follow a body out of the room it bred in.

**`cave_skitter`.** A small eight-legged crawler, in packs of two to four, bodied in fibre: old cord, sacking and timber where people worked, and the white thread that fungus puts through rock where they did not. It leaves `fiber`. Habit: the pack scatters from a body that turns on it and comes back from another side.

## 7.4 The sand-country fighters

Three people (5.3). They are born, so they die where they are killed. They come with the `ashen` march. Once `boss:2` is dead a few also follow the dusk packs in, to reach a store while the pack has the crew busy. In play that begins after any two first kills, whichever they are, and the cause asks for `boss:2` (chapter 8, changes). They want the stores, which they would break and burn, and they want the hunting stopped. The coin on a fallen fighter is lees taken on the way and carried for the fire. They are grey with the ash of their own fires.

They hit harder and take more killing after each first kill. That belongs to the game (1.9). The game also draws them with lit eyes and draws `siege_brute` two and a half strides tall. People have neither, and the art should change.

**`ashen_raider`.** A fighter with a blade. He carries `ash_token` and sometimes iron. Habit: he carries a lit brand in the off hand, so a march is seen before the light is up.

**`ashen_hexer`.** A fighter who throws fire. He carries a long iron with a basket head packed with pitch and `ember_dust`, the tool his people use to burn a carcass where it lies, and he throws burning pitch from it at thirteen strides. He carries tokens. The crystal sometimes found on him was taken from a store on the way, for the fire, like the lees. Habit: he stands off and does not close.

**`siege_brute`.** The breaker: the biggest of them, in thick padding, with a beam for doors and chests. He comes late in a march or in a large one. He carries the most tokens, and iron. Habit: he walks at the nearest door.

## 7.5 What forms on a totem

Every totem carries the same food: two carved bones with raw remains of the shade-kind bound on (6.6). One of the lowest feeders forms on it. It has no shape of its own, and it takes its body from the totem's metal. So there are nine looks and one kind of animal. It stays by the hand that carries its food, goes for other feeders because they are food to it, and is gone when the bearer goes down. Nothing binds it.

Its look follows the metal. No closer cause is claimed for any one look.

| Key | Totem head | What is seen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m_thornling` | Iron | A green lump with a thorn |
| `m_wisp` | `lumenite` | A point of the gem's colour |
| `m_brazier` | `cinder` | An ember in the air |
| `m_shade` | `umbrite` | A small dark flier |
| `m_mycelid` | `noctrium` | A small lit fungus cap |
| `m_zephyr` | `aether` | A small white flier |
| `m_shardling` | `prism` | A lit splinter |
| `m_moon` | `eclipse` | A dark four-legged thing with pale tines, a hand high |
| `m_cairn` | A lamp from the housing of an eighth stone (4.10) | A point of yellow light |

Habit for all nine: when no feeder is near, it settles on the totem and feeds.

## 7.6 Night, the moon and the three attacks

### Night

Night is the sun gone from the sky of the living ground. Then every unlit place is dark, and feeders come up in the open as they do under rock at all hours. Four kinds are seen only then: `gloomshade` everywhere, `frost_stalker` on cold ground, `cinder_wisp` on sand, `crag_bat` over the high rock. Householders stay within a step and a half of their beds, with a hand on the stone. Dusk and dawn are the two edges at which attacks begin.

What a crew sees coming, *in play*: the sun low on one side and the moon coming up on the other; the animals gone from open ground; the townsfolk gone in. *Proposed:* the `gobblet`s stop calling, the `woolback`s walk to the lamps, and the scrapes of the `burrowhog`s are stopped.

### The moon

The moon rises as the sun sets. A round dark crosses its face in a round of eight nights: whole on the fourth, wholly dark on the eighth (1.3). It is the only calendar.

The face does one thing on the ground. The deep kind bears no light at all, and its strongest comes up at its stones only on the night the moon is dark (1.6). So the moon tells a crew how many nights are left. After `boss:3` is dead, a dark moon means tonight.

### `raid`

Packs going for a store at dusk. In play it can happen once `boss:1` is dead, or once two householders are housed. It never falls on the dark night while the deep kind comes up as a host. On any other dusk the chance is about one in five.

Three in five of those that come are `gloomshade`, and a quarter are `thornling`. The woods and open country were the ground of `boss:1`. Its own kind fed there, and the shade-kind came up onto it each night on sufferance and left its food alone. With `boss:1` dead the stores on that ground belong to nothing, and everything that walks it at night goes for them: its own broken packs and the kind that comes to anything dead. Sand-country fighters come in behind them (7.4).

They come up in twos and threes, eighteen to twenty-eight strides out, and go on coming until enough are killed or until dawn. While it lasts the held hearths turn nothing (1.6).

At first light the pack goes down together, before the sun is high enough to undo it. A lone `gloomshade` has nothing to follow and stays feeding until the sun finds it. That is why the shades of a raid are never seen coming apart and the shades of an ordinary night are.

What a crew sees coming. *In play:* nothing before the first group. *Proposed:* in the last light the animals nearest the houses all run one way, away from where the first group will come up, and the `gobblet`s near the houses go silent together. Also *proposed:* the chance rises with great remains in chests and with forges and gates standing near the beds, since stores draw (chapter 8).

### `ashen`

A march of sand-country people on the crews' houses. It can happen once `boss:2` is dead. It begins at first light and ends at dusk, when they turn for home because they hold no wards. It never comes sooner than the third dawn after the last one: the walk out and back, and the burning of what was taken, fill the days between. On a dawn when it can come, the chance is one in five.

More than half are `ashen_raider`, about a third `ashen_hexer`, and the rest `siege_brute`. They are people, so no ward turns them, and the hearths are open while they are on the ground. A beaten march leaves behind the lees it had taken and the tokens its fighters carried. `deserter` is one of them who stayed.

What a crew sees coming. *In play:* nothing before the banner. *Proposed:* before dawn, a line of brands on the side the sand country lies, and smoke where the march rested.

### `moon`

The deep kind on the surface, on the dark night, from the night after `boss:3` is killed (1.6). `moonspawn` come up on the ground and `lunar_howler` in the air, in such numbers that the sky takes their colour. More of everything is up, about twenty hostile things near a person where there would be fourteen. No pack goes for a store that night, because the deep kind is stronger than any pack and the ground is its own. It does not happen in the hollow or on the islands, and it does not begin while one of the eight is up. It ends at dawn. The sun undoes whatever is still standing.

What a crew sees coming. *In play:* a word from `wayfinder` on about one visit in seven, and the violet coming into the sky at dusk. Once the drawn moon agrees with the cause (chapter 8, changes), the moon's face on the evenings before. *Proposed:* the first `lunar_howler` is heard before the light has gone.

## 7.7 What a crew knows

*Proposed.* The game has no bestiary. If it gets one, it is what a crew knows. A crew is people who agreed to work the same stones. What one of them learns over a carcass is told to the rest, as the Guide is told, and an arrival who comes up late is told all of it by whoever is there. So there is one for the server, and a new player opens it filled as far as the others have got.

It fills in four steps, each a thing a person could have come to know.

- **First sight.** What it looks like and when it is about. Householders can say this much of the common kinds, having watched from the hearths.
- **First kill.** What was inside it. Only someone who has opened a carcass over the pot knows that, so this step is always an arrival's.
- **Many kills.** What the kind does as a rule: its habit, as given in this chapter.
- **One thing seen once.** A fact that shows a cause, with where it was seen. The entries above hold several: the long bone in a `gloomshade`, the absence of any bone in a `rustbound`, a `frost_stalker` lying in full sun, feeders stopping at the water's edge.

Chapter 8 gives the form of an entry.

## 7.8 Older animals that hold one place

*Proposed.* A feeder with no lasting shape cannot grow old in one place. A kind that holds a body can, in two ways. Feeders are drawn to remains of their own kind, and stores and nests are such places. And once a host is broken each pack follows a strongest of its own, which grows on what it eats. These are the grounds for a larger, older animal of an ordinary kind that holds one place. The second sort is absent until the first kill of its host.

## 7.9 Open

- No feeder comes up in water. The cause given in 3.1 is that water has nothing set in it to take a body from. Nothing more is claimed.
- `boss:8` can be called by day, and the `moonspawn` that come to it would then stand in full sun.
- A `ruin` nest puts out `gloomshade`s by day. Under open sky each comes apart at the wall. This is true to the cause and should be left.
- Display names that state a wrong belief or a spell, for the naming pass: the sentinel, the knight, the hexer, the bound shade, the spirit, the sprite.
