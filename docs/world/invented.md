# Invented

Every element made for the game, one row each: what it is, why the game needed it, which borrowed element it sits beside (see `borrowed.md`), and whether it is in play. Invented elements may be changed, with a reason.

Rows marked **stretch** push a borrowed element further than its source states. They are the first to revisit if the source changes.

*In play* means the game shows it today. *Proposed* means it follows from a cause in the bible and is listed in chapter 8. *Frame* means it is a fact of the world that play rests on and never shows directly.
